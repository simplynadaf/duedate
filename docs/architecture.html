📬 DueDate: how a notice becomes a cited, trustworthy answer
Facts are code. The AI model only narrates. Every claim is pinned to a line of your own notice.
INPUT

📄 Upload notice

Amazon CloudFront + S3

Tenant pastes or uploads the eviction notice. Static page over HTTPS, no login, so judges and the AI scorer reach it in one click.

›
EXTRACT

🔎 Read every line

Amazon Textract

Each line is read with its position and confidence. This becomes the citation substrate every answer points back to.

Model freedom: none
›
ANALYZE

⚙️ Facts by rule

Deterministic engine

Notice type, amount, and deadline found by rules. Day-counts become dates by a shown rule. The model cannot change a date.

Model freedom: none
›
NARRATE

💬 Plain language

Amazon Bedrock (Nova Lite)

Translates and simplifies the facts into English or Spanish. Any sentence without a fact id is dropped before display.

Fenced: facts in, citations out
›
ROUTE

🤝 Free help

Legal-aid directory

Real free legal-aid links, chosen by the detected state. DueDate explains and cites, then hands off. Not legal advice.
